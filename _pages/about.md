Martin Metodiev is pursuing a postdoc (Onderzoeker 3) in the programme group Psychological Methods of the Faculty of Social and Behavioural Sciences at the University of Amsterdam. His research focuses on Bayesian statistics, particularly its computational and philosophical aspects. His work has addressed topics such as the evaluation of the marginal likelihood, the estimation of large covariance matrices, and the uniqueness of the Bayesian updating rule.
